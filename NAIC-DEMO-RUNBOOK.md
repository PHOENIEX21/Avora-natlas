# AVORA × N-ATLAS — 3–5 Minute Demo Runbook

## Demonstration goal
Show a real learner problem being solved end-to-end, with N-ATLAS materially enabling voice access rather than appearing as a decorative integration.

## Flow
**0:00–0:25 — Problem.** Briefly show why Nigerian learners need a low-friction voice-first learning path.

**0:25–0:55 — AVORA context.** Sign in as a learner and show the learner's real class/exam route, curriculum and authored lesson structure.

**0:55–1:45 — N-ATLAS voice.** Open Ask AVORA, ask a genuine curriculum question by voice, show the visible N-ATLAS transcript and N-ATLAS status.

**1:45–2:35 — Learning response.** Show AVORA answering from the appropriate curriculum/context source. Ask a natural follow-up to demonstrate continuity. If the transcript is wrong, demonstrate the correction path rather than hiding the error.

**2:35–3:15 — Learning outcome.** Complete a relevant exercise/mastery check where available and show that learning evidence is connected to the interaction.

**3:15–4:15 — Technical evidence.** Show the protected admin N-ATLAS evidence dashboard: genuine validation count, sessions, success/failure, latency, corrections, answer provenance, mastery and feedback. Do not display private learner content.

**4:15–5:00 — Validation and impact.** Show validation-only export/snapshot and state measured results only. Briefly explain the text-first/low-bandwidth design and how AVORA can scale across more verified curriculum while keeping N-ATLAS central to voice access.

## Recording rules
Use the actual working product. Do not substitute mock screens for failed runtime steps. Do not claim 50 interactions until the validation dashboard proves it. Preserve visible failures truthfully if they occur and re-record only after fixing the underlying problem.
